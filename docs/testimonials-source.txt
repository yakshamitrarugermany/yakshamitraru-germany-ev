1. Vasudeva Nayak Kukkundoor | Audience | Munich, Germany
I have had the pleasure of following the Yakshagana performances by Yakshamitraru, led by Mr. Apoorva Beleyur and his talented friends, for the past three to four years through various cultural programs organized by Kannada groups in Munich.

What makes their Yakshagana performances truly special is the beautiful presentation of stories from Hindu mythology, brought to life through vibrant costumes, colorful characters, expressive acting, and, above all, the wonderful use of the Kannada language. For me, this combination of mythology, traditional art, theatre, and Kannada creates a beautiful cultural experience that I thoroughly enjoy every time.

Mr. Beleyur, in particular, has an excellent command of Kannada, with impressive pronunciation and delivery, complemented wonderfully by his acting skills. His portrayal of the mighty Raavana in Lankadahana remains unforgettable. The energy, expressions, dialogue delivery, and sheer presence he brought to the character were simply outstanding. That performance made me an instant fan!

It is heartening to see Yakshamitraru bringing such a rich traditional art form to audiences far away from its roots. Through their performances, they are not only keeping the wonderful tradition of Yakshagana alive but also introducing our mythology and culture to new generations and helping preserve and popularize the Kannada language.

I sincerely hope and wish that Mr. Beleyur and the entire Yakshamitraru team continue to perform many more such programs across the world, taking the beauty of Yakshagana, Kannada, and our cultural heritage to wider audiences while continuing their service to society.

“Sarve Janāḥ Sukhino Bhavantu” — May everyone, everywhere, be happy and prosperous.

3. Dr. Anoosha N. Shastry | Fellow artist | Ayurveda Physician (BAMS, RGUHS - India), Bharatanatyam Artist; Founder & Director, Sanatana Akademie | Munich, Germany
Watching Yakshamitraru Germany has always meant more to me than simply watching a Yakshagana performance in Germany. Living and working with Indian knowledge traditions away from India, I know how delicate the balance is between making a tradition accessible to a new audience and allowing it to remain unmistakably itself. This is something I deeply appreciate in the work of Yakshamitraru Germany.

What stands out in their performances is how completely they bring the world of Yakshagana onto the stage. The music, costumes, movement, dialogue, storytelling and unmistakable energy of the art form come together without being diluted for a different audience. For those of us who know Yakshagana, there is an immediate familiarity and connection to home; for someone encountering it for the first time, it is an opportunity to experience the richness and character of the tradition in its authentic form.

Perhaps that is what I value most about their work: they are not only taking Yakshagana to new stages, they are allowing Yakshagana to travel while retaining its authentic identity.

My heartfelt appreciation to Apurva ji and the entire team of Yakshamitraru Germany for the dedication, conviction and immense effort behind each performance, and for continuing to give this treasured art form the space, respect and visibility it deserves.
